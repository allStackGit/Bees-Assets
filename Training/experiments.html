Training efficiency · Speed and bottleneck signals by curriculum stage

Effective steps/s = observed learner-step increase divided by observed training time. Paused evaluations and checkpoint transitions are excluded; the first partial interval after a restart is not counted. PPO busy uses the learner's cumulative optimizer clock. The PPO update, CPU and GPU columns sample the latest completed update; CPU/GPU and detailed timing require existing PPO diagnostics. Missing readings remain blank. These are screening signals, not definitive bottleneck diagnoses.

Checkpoint evaluations · What can this network do?

At each curriculum checkpoint, the saved network fights three fixed opponents, 100 matches each. The opponents and test conditions stay the same across beta runs. These matches measure ability; they do not train the network.

Compare checkpoints and beta runs

Compare the same test and stage across beta runs. Compare one network's earlier and later checkpoints to check learning and retention. A changed Unity build, opponent or benchmark recipe is a different test, even if the name is the same.
